# CMH Social Club: Friendsgiving (Simple v2)

*Draft for Brian · Oct 7, 2026 · replaces the fuller v1 concept for the first gathering*

---

## The idea in one line
**Our first CMH Social Club gathering: a Friendsgiving dinner that shows the Community pillar instead of just talking about it.**

## Why Friendsgiving
Community is Pillar 5. We teach it in the Starter Kit ("Build Your Corner"), but we've never *shown* it. Friendsgiving is already about the people you choose. That makes it the natural first gathering.

## Name
**CMH Social Club: Friendsgiving**
Tagline: *"Every fight needs a corner. Come meet yours."*

## The night (keep it simple)
- **Who:** about 20 invited guests. A mix of the CMH family (Anthony, the team, ambassadors), our partners, and high performers we'd love to bring closer.
- **Where:** a private dining room or a host's home [CONFIRM]
- **When:** the weekend before Thanksgiving [CONFIRM date]
- **Format:** arrival drinks (with great zero-proof options for everyone), then dinner at one shared table, then dessert and slow goodbyes. About 3 hours.

## Three simple moments
1. **Welcome (2 min):** Anthony or Brian. *"This is the Community pillar. Tonight we're not teaching it. We're living it."*
2. **The Corner Toast:** raise a glass to someone who's been in your corner this year. Anyone can share. No one has to.
3. **One question at the table:** *"Who's been in your corner this year, and do they know it?"*

That's it. Good food, real conversation, no pitch.

## Feel
Warm, elegant and unhurried. Candlelight, a long table, real plates, and minimal branding (one small CMH touch, like the place cards). Phones away at the table.

## Takeaway
A **Build Your Corner** card at each seat (from the Starter Kit), so guests leave with the Community pillar in their pocket.

## Afterward
- A personal thank-you from Brian to every guest within 48 hours.
- One newsletter story about the night (no guest names without permission).
- If it works: the CMH Social Club becomes a seasonal gathering.

## The pitch (say it out loud)
> "Community is one of our five pillars, and I want our first CMH Social Club to show it, not just teach it. A simple Friendsgiving: about twenty people, one table, good food, and a toast to the people in our corners. No fundraising, no pitch. Just CMH doing what it says it does. I need a date, a place and a budget, and Pamela and I will handle the rest."
