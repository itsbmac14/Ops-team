*Prepared by Brian McFadden, CMH Program Director · Oct 2026 · Draft for the team*

## Part 1: What the CMH Social Club is

**The CMH Social Club is our Community pillar in real life: curated gatherings where the people in the CMH world connect, support each other and practice what we teach.**

### Why we're building it
We teach five pillars: Mindset, Movement, Nutrition, Recovery and Community. Four of them can be taught through a screen. **Community can't.** You have to be in the room.

Our own Starter Kit says it best: *"Every fight needs a corner. You have one on fight night. You need one for life, too."* The Social Club is where CMH helps people build that corner.

It also answers something every high performer quietly knows: the higher you climb, the lonelier it can get. The Social Club is a place where performance and well-being sit at the same table.

### What it does for CMH
- **Lives our mission.** Connection is one of the most protective things for mental health. This is CMH providing it, not just talking about it.
- **Builds our community.** Fighters, ambassadors, partners and the high performers we're growing toward meet as peers.
- **Strengthens relationships.** Partners experience CMH at its best, with no pitch.
- **Feeds everything else.** Podcast guests, ambassadors and stories grow out of real relationships.

### Who it's for
The CMH family, our partners, and the high performers and like-minded people we want to bring closer. Gatherings are **invitation-based** to start. The way in is **character and shared values, not status.**

### What it is, and what it isn't
**It is:** intimate, warm, elegant and real.
**It isn't:** a fundraiser, a sales dinner, networking for its own sake, or a boxing-only event.

### How we gather
- **People first.** Everyone is a person before a title.
- **No pitch, ever.** Relationships are the point.
- **Small on purpose.** Around 20 people, so everyone actually connects.
- **Everyone feels welcome.** Great zero-proof options always, and no one has to share anything they don't want to.
- **Privacy respected.** Phones away at the table. Photos only with consent.
- **Practice, not lecture.** One simple Community moment per gathering.

## Part 2: Our first gathering, Friendsgiving

### Why Friendsgiving
Friendsgiving is already about the people you choose. That makes it the natural way to open the Social Club, and to **show** the Community pillar instead of just teaching it.

**CMH Social Club: Friendsgiving**
*"Every fight needs a corner. Come meet yours."*

### The details
- **When:** the weekend before Thanksgiving [CONFIRM date]
- **Where:** a private dining room or a host's home [CONFIRM venue and city]
- **Guests:** about 20, by invitation. The CMH family, our partners and high performers we'd love to bring closer.
- **Length:** about 3 hours

### The flow of the night
1. **Arrival:** drinks and introductions, with great zero-proof options for everyone
2. **Welcome (2 min):** *"This is the Community pillar. Tonight we're not teaching it. We're living it."*
3. **Dinner at one shared table:** good food and real conversation
4. **The Corner Toast:** raise a glass to someone who's been in your corner this year. Anyone can share. No one has to.
5. **The question at the table:** *"Who's been in your corner this year, and do they know it?"*
6. **Dessert and slow goodbyes**

### The feel
Warm, elegant and unhurried. Candlelight, a long table and minimal branding. Phones away at the table.

### What guests take home
A **Build Your Corner** card from our Starter Kit at every seat, so they leave with the Community pillar in their pocket.

### After the night
- A personal thank-you to every guest within 48 hours
- One newsletter story about the night (no names without permission)
- A team debrief on what worked, so the next gathering is even better
