## The core idea: one corner, two moments

CMH's whole identity already lives in one line: **"Every fight needs a corner."**
Every round of a fight has two moments where the corner matters:

1. **The fight itself.** The long work, where who you are gets tested. → **The Podcast** (long-form, deep, human)
2. **The 60 seconds between rounds.** The corner leans in with one clear instruction, a sip of water and a reset. → **The Newsletter** (short, weekly, one idea, one rep)

So the two products aren't separate brands. They're **two moments of the same corner.**

| | Podcast | Newsletter |
|---|---|---|
| **Name (recommended)** | **In Your Corner** *(a CMH Movement podcast)* | **Between Rounds** *(from CMH Movement)* |
| Role in the fight | The conversation | The reset |
| Promise | Real people on what it takes to flourish under pressure | One idea and one rep to carry into your week |
| Length | 45–60 min | 4-minute read |
| Cadence | Weekly or biweekly [CONFIRM] | Weekly |
| Feeling | Intimate, honest, curious | Clear, calm, actionable |

## 1. Podcast — **In Your Corner**

### Positioning statement
> For athletes, builders and anyone carrying weight no one sees, **In Your Corner** is the CMH Movement podcast where fighters, founders, artists and protectors tell the truth about what it takes to flourish under pressure, round by round across body, mind and community. Unlike performance shows that only chase the win, we ask about the whole person.

### Tagline options
- **"Real conversations about what it takes to flourish under pressure."** (recommended)
- "Behind every fighter is a person."
- "Well-being is part of performance."

### The format: **Five Rounds** (the Structured Freedom model, built into the show)
Every episode is structured as a five-round fight. It's the CMH model made audible, and it gives every episode the same spine, so clips, carousels and Skool tie-ins are predictable.

| Segment | What happens | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Walkout** (cold open) | The single most human 30–60 seconds of the episode | 0:00–1:00 |
| **Tale of the Tape** | Brian introduces the guest: who they are beyond the résumé | 1–3 min |
| **Round 1 · Mindset** | Pressure, self-talk, setbacks, the inner corner | ~8 min |
| **Round 2 · Movement** | How they train, and what movement does for their head | ~6 min |
| **Round 3 · Nutrition** | Fuel, weight cuts, the relationship with food | ~6 min |
| **Round 4 · Recovery** | Sleep, rest, the off-season, the hard stretches | ~6 min |
| **Round 5 · Community** | Who's in their corner, who they show up for | ~8 min |
| **The Final Bell** (signature closer, every guest) | *"What does it mean to flourish?"* (the North Star question) and *"Who's in your corner?"* | 3–5 min |
| **The Corner's Note** | Brian's 60-second takeaway plus this month's Skool course CTA | 1 min |

*Not every round needs equal time. Lean into the 2 rounds where the guest has the most to give. Always run the Walkout and the Final Bell.*

### Guest corners (the four sectors from the ecosystem model)
Every guest comes from one of four corners, each with a color code for thumbnails:
- **The Ring (Sport):** fighters, athletes, coaches. Includes every CMH ambassador.
- **The Arena (Business):** founders, executives, executive ambassadors.
- **The Studio (Art):** creators, musicians, filmmakers.
- **The Line (Tactical/Service):** first responders, military, clinicians, Thrive providers.

### Episode types
- **Fighter episodes:** ambassadors and the CMH card (program commitment)
- **Corner episodes:** experts (Dr. Mirhom, Marianne Girges, Thrive clinicians, CMH Network partners)
- **Course companion episodes:** each month, one episode goes deeper on that month's Skool course (example: an October episode on self-talk with a coach or sport psych guest)
- **Founder episodes:** Anthony, Kyle and the team on the mission and what's next

### Host presence
Brian is the guide, not the star (StoryBrand). The guest is the hero, Brian is the cornerman. Voice: calm, curious, warm, grounded. Asks the follow-up nobody else asks. Credits openly.

### Signature lines
- Open: *"Welcome to In Your Corner. I'm Brian McFadden. Every fight needs a corner, and today we're in ___'s."*
- Close: *"Whoever's in your corner, make sure you're in theirs. This is In Your Corner, from CMH Movement."*

## 2. Newsletter — **Between Rounds**

### Positioning statement
> **Between Rounds** is CMH Movement's free weekly letter: the 60 seconds in the corner for anyone in a fight of their own. One idea, one rep and one reminder that you don't have to do it alone.

### Tagline options
- **"One idea. One rep. Back in the fight."** (recommended)
- "The 60 seconds that change the next round."
- "Your weekly corner."

### Issue structure (renames the sections in `knowledge/programs/newsletter.md`)
| Section | What it is | Length |
|---|---|---|
| **The Bell** | Brian's lead essay: observation → universal struggle → reframe → tool → soft invitation | 300–600 words |
| **The Rep** | One tool from this month's Skool course or the Starter Kit, doable in 24 hours | ~100 words |
| **In the Corner** | Ambassador or fighter spotlight | ~120 words |
| **Ringside** | This week's podcast episode, one pull quote and the link | ~75 words |
| **CMH Cares Scorecard** | De-identified impact (sessions funded, athletes supported) and the giving link | ~50 words |
| **Presented by** | One disclosed, aligned sponsor | ~50 words |
| **Last Round** | One line to carry into the week, plus the 988 footer | 1–2 lines |

### Subject-line style
Short, a reframe or a curiosity gap, never hype:
- "The loudest voice in your corner"
- "You don't rise to the occasion"
- "What fighters know about Mondays"

### Voice
Brian at his clearest: the voice from The Art of Self-Talk intro. "Here's something most people never think about…" → "The good news?" → a concrete tool → "Let's get to work."
