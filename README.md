# CMH Program Director's Team

Brian McFadden's AI team for running CMH Movement's Podcast, Skool and courses, Newsletter, Thrive partnership (CMH Cares) and Ambassador Program.

## Meet the team
| | Role | Superpower |
|---|---|---|
| 🎬 **Content Director** | Runs the editorial machine | One monthly theme across all 5 programs. Briefs, reviews, priorities. |
| 🧠 **Founder Brain** | Thinks and writes like Brian | Casts vision around your ideas in your 3 V's: Values, Voice, Vibe |
| 📐 **Idea Architect** | Turns bullets into blueprints | North Star check → architecture → owners → 30/60/90 → guardrails |
| 🌊 **Content Waterfall Architect** | Multiplies every asset | 1 episode → 10–13 pieces a week across every channel and program |

## How to use it
Open this repo in Claude Code and type:
- `/weekly-plan`: your week across all five programs
- `/sharpen <brain dump>`: a raw idea becomes a blueprint
- `/vision <idea>`: a vision memo, pitch and headlines in your voice
- `/waterfall <episode / event / lesson>`: the full content cascade
- `/course <topic>`: next month's Skool course, built on the Self-Talk formula
- `/newsletter`: this week's issue
- `/episode <guest>`: podcast prep (Five Rounds question map)
- `/review <draft>`: SHIP / REVISE / KILL

Or just talk: "Have the founder brain write the November course intro."

## What the team knows (`knowledge/`)
- `cmh-bible.md`: mission, Structured Freedom pillars, people, network, rules
- `brian-voice-profile.md`: your voice, trained on your own writing
- `programs/`: playbooks for Podcast, Skool and courses, Newsletter, Thrive, Ambassadors
- `open-loops.md`: what's in flight
- `voices/`: 3 V's profiles for ambassadors and guests

Deliverables land in `outputs/`. First one: `outputs/2026-10-06-identity-podcast-newsletter.md`.
