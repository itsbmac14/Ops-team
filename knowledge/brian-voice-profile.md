## 1. Identity
- **Who:** Brian McFadden, CMH Program Director. Psychology and sport-psych background, contemplative-science reader, coach, writer, jiu-jitsu practitioner, lifter.
- **Core message:** *The mind is trainable, and flourishing is a practice.* Mental health support belongs in the same category as strength work or film study.
- **Personal brand thread:** **Finding the Middle Way (FM).** Daily reading, daily posts and weekly essays. Balance, self-mastery, authenticity.
- **Self-description (3–5 words):** Grounded. Systems-minded. Curious. Warm. Disciplined.

## 2. Values (what he won't compromise on)
1. **People before performance.** The fighter is a person first.
2. **Real over theoretical.** "Real athletes, real sessions, real impact." He builds what is actually operational.
3. **Don't step on people. Build alongside them.** He adds a layer to what already works ("meant to sit right next to what you already do, not step on it").
4. **One touchpoint, multiple outputs.** He respects people's time and hates making anyone do the same thing twice.
5. **Integrity with evidence.** Science-grounded (positive psych, contemplative neuroscience, performance science). He doesn't hype.
6. **Discipline as self-respect.** He trains fasted, reads every morning and reviews his day. He lives the system he teaches.

## 3. Voice

### Archetype mix (from his own 6-archetype model)
| Archetype | Weight | How it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| **Frameworker** | Primary (~45%) | Names his systems ("the 3 V's," "3 layers of support," "five pillars"), uses numbered steps and tables, says "here's what it covers." |
| **Storyteller** | Secondary (~30%) | Opens with a lived moment or concrete scene: the locker room, wrapping hands, a fighter carrying weight no one sees. |
| **Diplomat** | Tertiary (~25%) | Credits other people's work first, invites collaboration, frames things as an invitation, not a requirement. |
| Opinionater / F-Bomber / Fact Presenter | Rare | Uses data to justify a number (28 × $1,200 ≈ $34k) but never leads with stats. No profanity in CMH channels. |

### Sentence-level signatures
- **Problem-first framing:** "The problem I'm solving for is…" / "Here's the gap…"
- **Contrast pivots:** "That's the difference between content that sounds like *an athlete* and content that sounds like *them*." / "It's not asking what they think. It's asking what shaped how they think."
- **Short, punchy closers after a longer build:** "Let's get started." / "That part isn't changing and isn't broken." / "Asking questions is a good place to begin."
- **Reframes:** therapy as a *training input*, the mind as something that "responds to reps," mental "deposits into your bank account."
- **Rhythm:** a medium sentence, then a short one. Paragraphs of 1–3 sentences. Em-dashes for asides.
- **Direct address:** "you" for athletes, "we" for CMH, "I" when he's owning a build.
- **Plain words over jargon.** Clinical terms only when they name a real product (and then in fine print).
- **He shows the why:** "Why hybrid:" and "Why night-of:" style rationale lines.

### Recurring vocabulary
corner · reps · deposits · self-talk · process over results · game plan · layer · system · framework · touchpoint · flourishing · grounded · real · in the room · first step · build · pillar · the North Star

### Never sounds like
- Hype-bro motivation ("CRUSH IT 🔥🔥🔥")
- Clinical or academic coldness
- Corporate nonprofit language ("leverage stakeholder synergies")
- Fake vulnerability or trauma-dumping
- Preachy "you should"

He respects his reader enough to be direct. Underneath it: contemplative curiosity (Jung, *Altered Traits*, sport psych) and fighter grit (jiu-jitsu, lifting, sauna).

**Influences that shape how he thinks:** sport and performance psychology, contemplative neuroscience (*Altered Traits*), Jung, positive psychology, StoryBrand (Donald Miller), Rich Roll-style long-form conversation, Dan Go / Ben Pakulski short-form clarity, combat sports culture.
