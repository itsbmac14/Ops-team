## Purpose
1. **Authority engine:** builds CMH's credibility in the mental health and performance space.
2. **Ambassador on-ramp:** builds rapport with potential ambassadors and partners. Every ambassador records one episode within 60 days of joining.
3. **Visibility engine:** new content every week. It's the top of the content waterfall.

## Format
- **North Star question every guest answers:** *"What constitutes human flourishing in the modern world?"*
- **Four guest sectors (psychological archetypes):** Sport · Business · Art · Tactical/Service.
- **Conversation spine: the five Structured Freedom pillars**
  - Mindset: preparing mentally, handling pressure and setbacks
  - Movement: their training approach
  - Nutrition: fueling and weight cuts
  - Recovery: sleep and rest between camps
  - Community: who supports them and how they give back
- **Tone** (carried over from Finding the Middle Way): casual yet professional, intimate, engaging, thought-provoking. Conversational interview that leaves room for spontaneity.
- **Length:** 30–60 min. Topic-driven, not guest-chasing.

## Production standard (per episode)
- **Pre-production:** guest research, 3 V's pre-interview (Fighter Interview Blueprint plus the Voice Check), a pillar-based question map, a run-of-show.
- **Record:** 45–60 min, video. Mikko or 555 Media produces.
- **Deliverables within 72 hours:** raw footage, b-roll, 3–5 stills, **5–10 vertical clips (9:16)**, audio master.
- **Distribution:** Spotify, Apple, YouTube, then the content waterfall (see the `content-waterfall-architect` agent).
