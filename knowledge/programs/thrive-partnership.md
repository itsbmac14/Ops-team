# Program Playbook — Thrive Psychological Services Partnership (CMH Cares)

> Sources: *CMH-Thrive-Partnership-Outline Final* (Sept 9, 2026), CMH Cares campaign brief and founder summary, Thrive IG announcement, onboarding series scripts, Brian's daily log.

## What it is
Thrive Psychological Services is CMH's **Preferred Partner for Mental Health Support & Services**. Through **CMH Cares**, CMH fighters, alumni and their families get layered support:
1. **Therapy** (Thrive, clinical)
2. **Medication management** (Thrive, clinical)
3. **Mental skills coaching** (CMH: Brian and Dr. Mirhom, *not* Thrive)

## Confirmed terms
| Item | Term |
|---|---|
| Focus | General mental health wellness, *not* performance coaching |
| Who's covered | Any fighter with past, present or future CMH affiliation, **plus family** (spouse, children). Single, couples and family therapy offered. |
| When | Any stage: fight camp, pre-fight, post-fight, anytime |
| Model | Brief solution-focused therapy. **Market it as 8–10 sessions.** Clinically, 17–20 is typical. |
| Billing | Insurance first (Thrive handles verification). Out-of-pocket rate **$115/session**, which **CMH covers** for non-insured athletes. Thrive bills CMH, monthly preferred. |
| Pilot | **90 days**, cap of **6–8 concurrent CMH clients** |
| Delivery | Weekly, virtual or in person. Athletes pick a provider or get matched. |
| Intake | Send athletes to **Thrive's website** (intake already built). Any CMH-hosted form must be **HIPAA compliant** and mirror Thrive's fields. |
| Screening | One intake screening routes the athlete to the clinical lane or to performance referral (Dr. Mirhom) |
| Crisis | Case by case with the provider. Substance abuse, life-threatening violence and domestic abuse are **out of scope** (refer to emergency services, 988/911). |
| Confidentiality | CMH needs a signed **ROA (release of authorization)** from each athlete to receive any outcome data |
| Measurement | Treatment outcomes questionnaire → **BHI (Behavioral Health Index)** scores → **16-week outcomes report** |
| MOU | Not yet. Run the pilot first. |
| Contacts | Thrive: Mina (handing off to staff). CMH: **Brian**. |
| Marketing | "CMH partnering with Thrive. Preferred vendor." General marketing, pilot first. |

## Why the data matters
De-identified referral, engagement, completion and BHI data are CMH's **proof of impact** for grants, fundraising and sponsors. **Capture CMH-sourced leads** (UTM or referral code on the Thrive link, a "referred by CMH" intake field) without touching PHI.

## The athlete funnel
1. **Awareness:** IG announcement, CMH Cares landing page, ambassador kits (Thrive QR code), fight-week touchpoints, post-fight check-in.
2. **Pre-intake onboarding series** (4 videos, ~50 sec each, produced by Mikko). Throughline: **stigma → clarity → motivation.**
   1. *Welcome* (Anthony)
   2. *The Mind Is Trainable* (Dr. Mirhom): the 3 layers of support
   3. *What Happens in the Room* (Marianne Girges, LMFT): a first session demystified
   4. *Connecting to Performance* (Brian): mental reps, then the next step into the Thrive intake
3. **Thrive intake** → screening → clinical lane (Thrive) or performance lane (Dr. Mirhom / Brian).
4. **Weekly sessions** → outcome questionnaire → 16-week report.

## Copy rules

- Never imply Thrive does sport psychology or performance coaching.
- Never name or hint at an individual athlete's participation.
