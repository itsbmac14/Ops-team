## In one sentence
**The CMH Social Club is CMH's Community pillar in real life: curated in-person gatherings where the people in the CMH world connect, support each other and practice what we teach.**

## Why it exists
CMH teaches five pillars: Mindset, Movement, Nutrition, Recovery and Community. The first four can be taught through a screen. **Community can't.** You have to be in the room.

Our own Starter Kit says it: *"Every fight needs a corner. You have one on fight night. You need one for life, too."* The Social Club is where CMH helps people build that corner.

It also answers something every high performer quietly knows: the higher you climb, the lonelier it can get. The Social Club is a place where performance and well-being sit at the same table.

## What it does for CMH
1. **Lives the mission.** It turns "Championing Mental Health" from content into an experience. Connection is one of the most protective things for mental health, and this is CMH providing it.
2. **Builds the community.** Fighters, ambassadors, partners, and the high performers and meaning-seekers we're growing toward (the Gervais/Brooks audience) meet as peers.
3. **Strengthens relationships.** Partners, sponsors and future collaborators experience CMH at its best, with no pitch. Trust comes first; everything else follows.
4. **Feeds the hub.** Stories, podcast guests, ambassadors and newsletter moments come out of real relationships, never forced.

## Who it's for
- **The CMH family:** founder, team, ambassadors and fighters
- **Our partners:** the CMH Network (Thrive, Motus PT, sponsors and others)
- **People we want to bring closer:** high performers in sport, business, the arts and service, and people who share our values
- **(Proposed) Over time:** members of the CMH Skool community and the broader public at open events

Gatherings are **invitation-based** to start, so each room is intentional. The gate is **character and shared values, not status or net worth.**

## What it is / what it isn't
**It is:** intimate, warm, elegant, real conversation, people as people, and Community-pillar practice.
**It isn't:** a fundraiser, a sales dinner, networking for its own sake, a party, or a boxing-only event.

## Principles (proposed)
1. **People first.** Everyone is a person before a title, including fighters, CEOs and partners.
2. **Care first, promotion second.** No pitch, ever. Relationships are the point.
3. **Small and intentional.** Around 20 people per gathering, so everyone actually connects.
4. **Everyone feels welcome.** Great zero-proof options always. No one has to share anything they don't want to.
5. **Privacy respected.** Phones away at the table. Photos only with consent. No guest named publicly without permission.
6. **Practice, not lecture.** Each gathering has one simple Community moment (a toast, a question, a card), not a presentation.

## What it could become (proposed)
- **Seasonal gatherings:** a Friendsgiving (first), then others around natural moments of the year
- **Local community service:** Anthony's notes pair the Social Club with service. Gatherings where we give back together.
- **Ambassador meetups:** a place for ambassadors to connect with each other and the community
- **Movement and Recovery socials:** a training session or recovery experience, then a meal
- **City chapters:** if it works, the model travels wherever CMH has people

## How it fits the CMH model
- **Pillar:** Community (Pillar 5 of Structured Freedom)
- **Ecosystem layer:** the Community layer, next to the future CMH Reset retreat and CMH Summit. The Social Club is the regular, local, intimate version; the Reset and Summit are the larger annual experiences.
- **Mandate:** brings in the broader audience in person, and shows high performance and well-being together

## How we'll know it's working
- Guests leave feeling more connected (a quick, optional follow-up)
- Repeat attendance and guest referrals to the next gathering
- New relationships: ambassadors, podcast guests, partners and Skool members who came through the Club
- Stories worth telling (shared only with permission)
