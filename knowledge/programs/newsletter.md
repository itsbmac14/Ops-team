# Program Playbook — CMH Newsletter

> Sources: CMH Ecosystem doc (Newsletter + Substack), the CMH business model draft (Aug 19, 2026), the 90 Day Content Plan's essay structure and Brian's FM essay practice.

> **Identity draft (Oct 6):** proposed name *Between Rounds*, "One idea. One rep. Back in the fight." Sections renamed The Bell / The Rep / In the Corner / Ringside / CMH Cares Scorecard / Presented by / Last Round. See `outputs/2026-10-06-identity-podcast-newsletter.md`. Pending Brian's approval.

## Role
- **Free tier of the offer ladder.** It's the owned audience CMH controls, not something rented from IG.
- **Revenue:** newsletter sponsorship (one aligned sponsor per issue).
- **Glue:** it's where every program reports back to the community. Podcast, Skool, Thrive/CMH Cares impact, ambassador spotlights, events.

## Format (weekly recommended; confirm cadence with Brian)
**Name (working):** Between Rounds *(proposed; see identity draft)*

| Section | Length | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **The Opening Round**: lead essay | 400–700 words | Brian (founder-brain), structured as observation → universal struggle → reframe → simple tool → soft invitation |
| 2. **In the Corner**: ambassador or fighter spotlight | 100–150 words | Ambassador Program |
| 3. **This Week's Rep**: one pillar tool | 75–125 words | Skool lesson or Starter Kit |
| 4. **On the Pod**: episode plus a pull quote | 50–100 words | Podcast |
| 5. **CMH Cares Impact**: de-identified numbers and a giving link | 50–75 words | Thrive data (aggregate only, with ROA) and campaign |
| 6. **Sponsor slot** | 40–60 words | Disclosed |
| Footer | — | 988 line · Skool link · podcast link · reply prompt |

## Editorial rules
- Subject line under 45 characters: a curiosity gap or a reframe, no clickbait.
- One primary CTA per issue (rotate: Skool, podcast, donate, Thrive intake, event).
- Written in Brian's voice (see `knowledge/brian-voice-profile.md`). Anthony-voice editions only for founder letters.
- Sensitive topics → the 988 line at the top as well as the footer.

## Growth loops
- Starter Kit gated by email, with an opt-in to the newsletter
- Podcast show notes → subscribe CTA
- Ambassador bios link to subscribe
- Event QR codes (fight nights, Social Club)
- Substack cross-post for discovery
