# Program Playbook — CMH Ambassador Program

> Source: *CMH Ambassador Program — Program Overview 2026* (deck Brian shared Oct 6, 2026), the CMH Ecosystem doc and Notes Anthony 9/30.

## Positioning
**"Every fight needs a corner."** CMH Ambassadors carry the corner into every fight, every post and every conversation.
**Purpose: Care first. Promotion second.** An athlete who feels supported by CMH will represent it naturally. An athlete who feels used will not.

Two goals carry equal weight:
1. **Connection:** a consistent touchpoint with every ambassador, including access to mental health support through Thrive.
2. **Exposure:** steady, authentic visibility for CMH Movement on fight nights, on Instagram and through the CMH Podcast.

**The ask: 15 minutes a month outside fight week.** CMH does the heavy lifting: content, captions, kits and scheduling.

## Who
- Opt-in athletes who compete on a CMH card or are part of the CMH community.
- **Founding cohort:** Noel (Mikaelian), Mai, Brooke.
- **Selection criteria:** competed on a CMH card or part of the community · shares CMH's values on mental health and performance · willing to post and tag a few times a month · open to a podcast conversation.
- **Follower count is not a requirement. Authenticity matters more than reach.**
- The long-term vision (Ecosystem doc) adds **Executive Ambassadors** (CEOs, founders, creators) as a second tier for corporate wellness credibility.

## The four elements
1. **Welcome Kit.** Ships within 2 weeks of joining. Contents: shirts and hat, stickers, shorts badge, handwritten note (Anthony or the team), Ambassador Card (mission, five pillars, the ask), **Thrive QR code**. Content ask: an unboxing video tagging @cmhmovement. It's their first post and our first ambassador content.
2. **Fight Week Package.** CMH badge on fight shorts · CMH shirt for weigh-ins, media day and walkout · pre-fight graphic from Mikko with captions written by CMH · post-fight story template (win or lose) · **post-fight check-in (most important).** Before badges ship, confirm logo rules with each athlete's team, promoter and commission.
3. **Tag @cmhmovement + Monthly Content Kit.**
   - CMH sends: one branded template (Mikko) · 2–3 captions **in the athlete's voice** · a simple film/photo prompt · that month's **Structured Freedom** pillar theme.
   - The athlete picks a caption, shoots one clip, drops it in the template, posts and tags.
   - Monthly pillar rotation: Mindset → Movement → Nutrition → Recovery → Community.
   - CMH gives back: reshare within 24 hours, plus a monthly Ambassador Spotlight on the main CMH page.
   - Ambassadors tag on: fight announcements and fight nights, pillar-tied training posts, CMH events/workshops/Social Club, and their podcast episode and clips.
4. **Podcast.** One episode per ambassador within 60 days of joining. The conversation follows the five Structured Freedom pillars (Mindset: pressure and setbacks · Movement: training approach · Nutrition: fueling and weight cuts · Recovery: sleep and rest between camps · Community: who supports them and how they give back). **1 recording → 5–10 clips cut by CMH → 1 month posted from their account.**

## The journey
| When | Stage | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Invitation | Personal invite and one-page agreement |
| Weeks 1–2 | Welcome Kit | Kit ships, unboxing post, optional Thrive intake |
| Weeks 3–8 | Podcast | Episode recorded and released, clips scheduled |
| Every fight | Fight Week | Badge, shirt, graphics, post-fight check-in |
| Every month | Rhythm | Content kit, one tagged post, monthly check-in |
| Every 6 months | Review | Quick conversation on what's working |

**Monthly check-in (3 questions):** (1) How are you doing, honestly, on a scale of 1–10? (2) What's coming up this month: fights, camps, life? (3) Is there anything CMH can help with?
Responses go **to the program lead only**. Anyone who is struggling gets offered a warm handoff to Thrive.

## The exchange
| Ambassadors commit to | CMH commits to |
|---|---|
| Badge on fight shorts | Welcome Kit and replacement gear |
| Tag @cmhmovement | Reshare every tagged post, monthly spotlight |
| Record one podcast episode | Professional episode and 5–10 clips |
| Post one content kit a month | Monthly graphics and captions in their voice |
| Complete the monthly check-in | Priority access to therapy through Thrive |
| Represent CMH's values | Sponsor introductions and paid appearances as they arise |
One-page plain-language agreement, renewable every 12 months. Either side can step away at any time.

## Metrics (reported quarterly to Anthony)
Active ambassadors · check-in response rate · Thrive referrals and intakes · tagged posts per month · episode and clip views · event appearances · @cmhmovement growth.

## Guardrails
- **Trunk logos:** confirm commission and promoter rules before every fight.
- **Sponsored posts:** clear disclosure (#ad / paid partnership label).
- **Peptide sponsor (Precision Peptide):** vet carefully given anti-doping and commission rules.
- **Check-in privacy:** never used for marketing. It stays with the program lead and Thrive.

## First 90 days
- **Days 1–30, Build:** confirm owners with Anthony · draft the agreement and ambassador card · order kits and badges with Mikko · set up the tracker, folder and check-in form · invite Noel, Mai and Brooke.
- **Days 31–60, Onboard:** ship kits and unboxing posts · record the first 3 episodes · send the first content kit and check-in · announce the founding ambassadors.
- **Days 61–90, Refine + Expand:** release episodes and clips on schedule · review with the cohort · invite the next group of CMH fighters · deliver the first quarterly report.

**Open questions:** per-ambassador kit budget · podcast release schedule · Kyle/Brian role split.

## Voice capture
Every ambassador gets a **3 V's voice profile** (Values, Voice, Vibe and one of the 6 archetypes), built from the podcast recording and the Fighter Interview Blueprint add-on questions. Keep profiles in `knowledge/voices/<athlete>.md`. Monthly captions are written from that profile, never from a generic "fighter" voice.
