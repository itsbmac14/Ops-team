### The CMH Course Formula (locked from Course 1; reuse every month)
- **4 lessons × ~5 min = ~20 min.** One idea and **one downloadable tool** per lesson.
- **Intro script:** who I am → the surprising truth (a reframe) → "the good news: it can be trained" → the promise (time, lessons, named experts) → the end-state artifact ("your own ___ playbook") → "ring, court, or a hard day" universality → "Let's get to work."
- **Lesson 1 = reframe** (skill, not trait). **Lessons 2–4 = one respected expert/book each** (credibility borrowed openly and credited).
- Lesson names are short and evocative, often fighter metaphors (*Inner Corner*, *Bank*, *The Space Between*).
- The tools stack into a **capstone artifact** in the last lesson.
- Audience line: "whether you're stepping into the ring, onto the court, or into a hard day."
- Every tool carries the 988 footer.
- Each course waterfalls: the intro becomes a reel, each lesson's big idea becomes a carousel, a podcast episode features one of the experts' ideas with a guest, and the newsletter runs four weeks of "This Week's Rep."

## Lesson standard (every Skool lesson)
```
LESSON TITLE (verb + outcome): ___
Pillar: ___ | Course: ___ | Lesson # ___ | Length: 5–10 min video + 1 tool
1. Hook (a fighter scene or a real moment, 2–3 sentences)
2. The idea (one concept, plain language, cite the science lightly)
3. The reframe ("not X, it's Y")
4. The tool (worksheet, checklist, protocol; one page)
5. The rep (what to do in the next 24 hours)
6. Community prompt (post your ___ in the thread)
7. Bridge (next lesson, plus the podcast episode that goes deeper)
Footer: the 988 safety line
```

## Course design principles
- **One lesson, one idea, one tool, one rep.**
- Pillar rotation matches the ambassador content kit (Mindset → Movement → Nutrition → Recovery → Community), so Skool, social and ambassador content share a monthly theme.
- Every lesson feeds the waterfall: a carousel, a newsletter section and a podcast question.
- Education only. Clinical needs route to Thrive, performance needs to mental skills coaching.
